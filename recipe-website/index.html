Bourak

A crispy, golden Algerian pastry rolled around a savory filling of minced meat, egg, and herbs — a classic favorite for Ramadan and family gatherings

Preparation Time

  • Prep time: 30 minutes
  • Cook time: 15 minutes
  • Total time: 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • dioul (brick) pastry sheets
  • ground beef (or lamb)
  • medium onion, finely chopped
  • handful fresh parsley, chopped
  • portions triangle cheese, or grated cheese
  • eggs
  • tbsp vegetable oil or butter
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • A pinch of cinnamon
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Heat 2 tbsp of vegetable oil (or butter) in a frying pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the finely chopped onion and cook for 3–4 minutes until soft and translucent.
  3. Add the ground beef (or lamb) and cook for 7–10 minutes, breaking it apart with a spoon until fully browned.
  4. Season with salt, black pepper, and a pinch of cinnamon. Stir well.
  5. Add the chopped fresh parsley and cook for 1 minute. Remove from the heat and allow the filling to cool completely.
  6. Mix one beaten egg into the cooled filling (optional, for a richer texture), or reserve the egg for sealing the pastry.
  7. Lay one dioul (brick) pastry sheet on a clean work surface. Place 2–3 tablespoons of the meat filling near one edge and add a portion of triangle cheese or grated cheese on top.
  8. Fold the pastry into a triangle or roll it into a cigar shape, sealing the edge with a little beaten egg.
  9. Repeat with the remaining pastry sheets and filling.
  10. Heat vegetable oil for frying in a deep frying pan over medium heat.
  11. Fry the bouraks in batches for 2–3 minutes per side, until golden brown and crispy.
  12. Transfer to paper towels to drain excess oil and serve hot.
